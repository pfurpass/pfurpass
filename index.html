Services

What the studio does

Six things, done properly. If your project needs something else, say so — we will tell you honestly whether we are the right studio for it.

Website design

Structure first, then layout, type and colour. You get pages built around what visitors came to do — call you, read a price, find the door.

Frontend development

Hand-written HTML, CSS and JavaScript, or a framework when it earns its place. No page-builder plugins to renew every year.

Design systems

Tokens, components and patterns your team can reuse — built on Clarity, or on the rules your brand already follows.

AI recognition systems

Image and document recognition wired into a real workflow: read the label, sort the file, fill the form, hand the result to a person.

Automation software

Small tools that take the repeat work off your desk: exports, reports, price lists, data clean-up, files moved between systems.

Care and maintenance

Updates, backups, uptime checks and the small changes that keep coming up. One address to write to when something looks wrong.

How we work

Five steps, in this order

The order matters: each step decides what the next one is allowed to assume. You approve the result of every step before we move on.

  1. 01

    Talk

    Half an hour on the phone or at the table in Farch. What the site has to achieve, who has to use it, and what your budget actually allows.

    No charge
  2. 02

    Structure

    Sitemap, page list and wireframes. We agree on what goes on each page before anyone argues about a shade of blue.

    Approved in writing
  3. 03

    Design

    Type, colour and components as a small system, then the key pages drawn from it. Two rounds of revisions are part of the price.

    Two revision rounds
  4. 04

    Build

    Clean markup, tested on real phones and real browsers, checked with a keyboard and a screen reader before it goes anywhere near your domain.

    Staging link for review
  5. 05

    Launch and look after

    We publish, hand over the files and a short guide, then keep an eye on it. Changes after launch are quoted per job or covered by a care plan.

    Handover included
